Sound contrast lab

Letters are not sounds

Learn why a Dutch word can look unfamiliar even when the IPA is simple. These are productive rules and frequent patterns—not a promise that Dutch spelling has no exceptions.

STEP 01

Observe

Study the IPA symbol, lips, tongue description and Greek comparison. Do not guess the sound from Dutch spelling.

STEP 02

Listen & imitate

Play a Dutch example, repeat it slowly three times, and compare it with another word from the same sound family.

STEP 03

Verify with people

Check native educational recordings or ask a teacher. Recording yourself helps, but this lab does not automatically score pronunciation accuracy.
